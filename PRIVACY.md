# Tsuzuki privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Tsuzuki is a reader for light novels, web novels, manga and manhwa. It has no accounts, no servers of its own and no
advertising. This page explains what the app does with your data.

## What stays on your device

Your library, shelves, reading progress, history, reading minutes and goals, notes, downloads and settings are stored
only on your device. Tsuzuki doesn't send them anywhere.

Backups are files that you create and choose where to save. If you save one to a cloud folder, that folder's provider
handles it under its own policy.

## What leaves your device, and only when you use it

- **Sources and extensions.** Browsing, searching, updating and downloading contact the websites of the sources you
  add, through the extensions or plugins you install. Those sites see a normal web request (your IP address and the
  pages asked for) and their own privacy policies apply. Extensions are written by third parties; install only ones
  you trust.
- **Extension repositories.** Tsuzuki downloads the repository lists you add (by default the LNReader plugin index and
  Keiyoushi) to show available extensions.
- **Trackers.** If you log in to a tracker (for example AniList or MyAnimeList), Tsuzuki sends your reading progress
  for the books you link to that service. You can log out at any time in Settings → Tracking.
- **Translation.** If you turn on translation and pick an online engine (Google, DeepL, Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek,
  Systran or a custom endpoint), the text being translated is sent to that service with the API key you provide.
- **Fonts.** Downloading a Google font in the font manager contacts Google Fonts.
- **Updates.** If update checks are on, Tsuzuki asks GitHub for the latest release of this repository.

## Analytics and crash reports

Tsuzuki's builds don't include analytics or crash reporting. If a future version adds opt-in crash reports, this page
will be updated before that version is released, and it will stay off until you turn it on.

## Children

Tsuzuki doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. Content comes from the sources you add; you're
responsible for the sources you use.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are recorded in this file's history. Questions: open an issue in this repository.

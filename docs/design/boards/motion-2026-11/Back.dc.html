WHILE THE FINGER IS DOWN
Page scale 1 → 0.90 and corners 0 → 28 dp with drag progress.
Page moves up to 24 dp toward the finger's side.
Screen behind: starts 30 dp left, 55 % brightness; moves and lightens with progress.
AFTER RELEASE
Commit: page leaves sideways in 250 ms (exit curve); behind settles at 0 dp, full brightness.
Cancel: back to full size in 250 ms (enter curve).
WHERE
Every pushed screen (details, settings, browse source, migration). Reader and player keep the system's own back-to-app animation.

RULE 1 · READY, THEN MOVE
A screen is built while the old one still shows; the move starts on the next frame. No frame over 8 ms once motion starts (120 Hz).
RULE 2 · NOTHING PUSHES
Late content fades in over 150 ms into space kept for it. Never "empty" before data answers. Lists, buttons and counts never shift the page.
RULE 3 · REDUCED MOTION
System animations off or the app's Reduce motion: every move becomes a 150 ms crossfade. No slides, scales or flying covers.

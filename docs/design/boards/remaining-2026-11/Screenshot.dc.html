Tapping closes nothing: a 150 ms white flash over the picture confirms the frame, the snackbar rises (300 ms, Enter) for 4 s. Reduced motion: no flash, the snackbar fades.
RULES
Place · options sheet, after Audio / Subtitles / Speed, before Open in another app.
File · Title - EP 12 - 00-14-32.png (title, episode, position), unsafe characters replaced.
Where · MediaStore Pictures/Tsuzuki/; written on IO; the cache copy is deleted after.
Share · opens the system share sheet with the saved image.
Fails · snackbar “Couldn't save the screenshot” in the same place, no Share.
Not offered · while the resume cover is still up (no frame yet).

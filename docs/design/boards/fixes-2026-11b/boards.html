Tsuzuki boards — batch 2026-11b

Four states the app shows today without a design. Drawn by Claude on 2026-11-12 from the tokens in docs/design/README.md; Dot on the left, Colour on the right. Phone frames are 360 dp wide, the tablet frame is the Tab S11 in portrait (753 dp), cropped to its lower part. All text is example copy except where README.md in this folder says it comes from data.

1 · ErrorState — a source or page failed

Replaces "Unknown error". Headline says what failed in plain words, one sentence says why, the dashed box shows the technical message for bug reports (tap copies it). At most two buttons: the most useful one is the accent pill.

2 · LoadingStages — a wait longer than two seconds

A bare spinner becomes a tile that names the step in progress. Finished steps are white, the current one carries the accent dot, later ones are muted. After 30 s on one step the tile turns into ErrorState with "Took too long".

3 · Recap — "previously" at the start of a chapter

Sits above the first paragraph and scrolls away with the text; it is never a page of its own, so it cannot move the resume position. Shows the last three paragraphs of the previous chapter, only after a gap of a day or more. The Dot selection colours for the reader are shown on the word "lantern".

4 · TabletListenDock — compact Listen controls with the sleep timer

The tablet in portrait (under 900 dp wide) gets the same controls as the phone's ListenMini board, including the timer. One row, 64 dp tall, 24 dp from the edges. The timer chip is outlined when off and accent-outlined with the time left when running.

5 · Voices — choose the reading voice and download the offline one

More › Settings › Reader › Voices, and the same page from the Listen panel's voice row. Two engines as tiles; the offline voice is a download, never part of the app. Left: before the download. Right: downloaded, with the voice list. A voice row plays a one-sentence sample when tapped; the selected row carries the accent mark.

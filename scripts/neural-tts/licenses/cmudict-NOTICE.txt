This product's English pronunciation lexicon was converted from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary
(cmudict.dict, github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict, commit 74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690).
The ARPAbet pronunciations were mapped to Kokoro phoneme symbols by scripts/neural-tts/build-lexicon-cmudict.py;
the data was modified by that conversion. The full CMU licence text is in cmudict-LICENSE.txt and must be
kept with any copy of the converted lexicon.
